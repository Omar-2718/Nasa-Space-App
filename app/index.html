The story

45 years under the sand

  1. 1981 The Space Shuttle's SIR-A radar flies over the Egypt–Sudan border and reveals river valleys buried under the Selima Sand Sheet.
  2. 2024 Radar and soil cores map the lost Ahramat branch of the Nile running past the pyramids of Giza.
  3. 2025 NASA and ISRO launch NISAR, with an L-band radar at almost the same wavelength as SIR-A.
  4. 2026 NISAR images this desert every 12 days, free for anyone.

Why it matters

  • Water: old riverbeds can hold groundwater and point to where to drill.
  • Archaeology: people lived along these rivers when the Sahara was green.
  • Climate: the channels record a wetter Sahara thousands of years ago.
